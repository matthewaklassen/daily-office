# The Daily Office

Morning and Evening Prayer after the Book of Common Prayer (1662), with the Scottish Psalter (1650), the Westminster Shorter Catechism and M'Cheyne's calendar of readings. Three forms: personal (about five minutes), family (about ten) and the full office.

A static site: `index.html` loads its texts from `data.json`. No build step.

Texts: BCP 1662 is public domain outside the United Kingdom; within the UK the rights are vested in the Crown. Acknowledgment: BCP 1662.
